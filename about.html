About me

Hi, I'm Grace. I'm a student at Brighton College, and this is where I put the things I write.

Most of what ends up here starts with something I got curious about — usually history, and usually the parts of a story that don't get told much. My first post is about the Chinese Labour Corps in World War I: thousands of workers who were essential to the war effort and then mostly forgotten.

Away from writing, I'm into music and sport, and lately I've been learning to code — which is how this site came about.

Nothing here is polished. That's kind of the point.
