The Hidden Army of Labour in World War I

July 21, 2026

The Chinese Labour Corps: The Hidden Workforce of World War I

When people hear about World War I, they usually think about soldiers, trenches, and battles in Europe. But there were also many people working behind the scenes. The Chinese Labour Corps was one of those groups. Daryl Klein wrote about them in With the Chinks, especially their training and journey. His book shows that these workers helped the war in a big way.

Who were the Chinese Labour Corps?

The Chinese Labour Corps were a group of men who were recruited to do hard labour for the Allied war effort. They were not mainly fighting in battles. Instead, they did important work to help the army. Klein explains that British officers like him were more like labour managers than army commanders. This shows us that war was not just about fighting — it was also about organizing people and doing practical work.

Training in China

One interesting part of the book is the training camp in China. Klein says the camp was a former silk-filature factory that had been turned into a training camp. That sounds very unusual, but it shows how people had to use whatever buildings they had during the war. He also describes the training as strict and organized, and he shows that British officers were more like labour managers than army commanders.

A very long journey

Another important part of the book is the Chinese Labour Corps' journey overseas from China to France. Klein calls it the "Interminable Journey." The route went through many places, including Canada, the Panama Canal, Jamaica, New York, and the Atlantic Ocean, which shows the general role and importance of the Corps in World War I. I think this is amazing because it shows how international World War I really was.

Breaking stereotypes

Klein also says that he wanted to challenge the bad ideas about "Chinese coolies" and stereotypes. This is important because it shows that he saw the Chinese workers as real people, not just stereotypes. That part of the book is especially interesting to me because it gives a different view of Chinese people at that time.

Why does this history matter?

The Chinese Labour Corps matter because they were part of the hidden support system of World War I. Without them, a lot of work would have been much harder. The book shows their recruitment, their training, and their journey overseas, so we can see how important they were in Source 1, 2, 7 and 8.

Conclusion

In conclusion, the Chinese Labour Corps were a very important but often the part that everyone will mainly forget of World War I. With the Chinks helps us understand their work, their long journey, and the way they were seen by others in Source 1, 2 and 5. This history is important because it reminds us that war is not only about soldiers — it is also about the many workers who support them.
