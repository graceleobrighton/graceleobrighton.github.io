On learning in public

July 12, 2026

There's a quiet pressure to only share things once they're polished — the finished project, the correct answer, the confident take. But most of the learning happens in the messy middle, and that's exactly the part we tend to hide.

Sharing work before it's finished does a few things. It invites help earlier, when help is cheap. It creates a record you can look back on. And it lowers the stakes: a rough note is easy to publish, and easy notes are the ones that actually get written.

A simple rule

When I'm unsure whether something is "ready," I ask: would a slightly younger version of me have found this useful? If yes, it ships.

That's the whole idea behind this blog. Less waiting, more writing.
