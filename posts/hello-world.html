Hello, world

July 20, 2026

For a long time I told myself I'd start writing "once I had something worth saying." That day never quite arrived, so I'm starting anyway.

This blog is a place for me to think out loud — about the things I'm building, the ideas I'm chewing on, and the occasional thing I get wrong. If any of it is useful to you, wonderful. If not, that's fine too; mostly I'm writing to clarify my own thinking.

What to expect

Roughly, posts here will fall into a few buckets:

  • Notes on projects I'm working on
  • Things I've learned and want to remember
  • Half-formed ideas I'm hoping to develop
Write the post you wish you'd found when you were figuring it out.

Thanks for reading. More soon.
